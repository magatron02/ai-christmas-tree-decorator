The first image is a photograph of a real bare Christmas tree in a shop. Return that same
tree with decorations added to it.

This is an edit of an existing photo, not a new picture of a similar tree. Preserve exactly:
- the tree itself — its height, width, silhouette, branch structure, species, how full or
  sparse it is, how it leans, where its gaps are
- its scale within the frame, the camera angle, the focal length
- the photographic character: the same grain, focus, white balance, exposure and colour

If the returned tree could be told apart from the tree in the first image with the
decorations removed, the result is wrong.

Placement
- Distribute copies over the whole tree: top, middle and bottom, left and right.
- Do not cluster them in one area and do not arrange them in a grid, ring or spiral.
- Hang each copy where a real decoration would sit: resting on or hanging from an existing
  branch, slightly tucked into the foliage, never floating in front of the tree or in mid-air.
- Vary the depth. Some copies sit closer to the trunk and are partly hidden by branches.
- Work around the tree that is there. Do not add, move or thicken branches to make a copy sit
  better.

- Copies further back are slightly smaller, copies at the front slightly larger; no other
  size variation between copies.

Lighting and material
- Add contact shadows where a copy touches a branch, and a soft cast shadow on the foliage
  behind it.
- Keep each decoration's own material: if it is glossy it picks up a highlight from the
  scene's light, if it is matte it does not.

Do not add any decoration that was not supplied. No extra ornaments, no lights, no ribbon,
no star, no tinsel, no snow, no gifts, no text, no watermark.
